## About This Opportunity

The **DAAD In-Country/In-Region Scholarship Programme** funds graduates from sub-Saharan Africa to do development-relevant master's degrees **within Africa**, in their home country or a neighbouring one, instead of travelling abroad. The programme is funded by the German government and the 2027 call includes several programmes in **Ghana**.

According to the 2027 call, the Ghana programmes are:

- **University for Development Studies (UDS):** Master of Philosophy in Agricultural Economics
- **West African Centre for Crop Improvement (WACCI), University of Ghana:** MPhil in Seed Science and Technology
- **West African Centre for Cell Biology of Infectious Pathogens (WACCBIP), University of Ghana:** MPhil in Molecular Cell Biology of Infectious Diseases

The programme particularly encourages applications from women, recognised refugees and candidates from marginalised regions or groups.

## Eligibility Requirements

- A national or permanent resident of a sub-Saharan African country, subject to each programme's call
- A strong, relevant bachelor's degree
- Meeting the host university's admission, degree-recency and language requirements
- Applying for one of the eligible programmes at the host institution

Read the programme-specific call for the exact subject, degree and age requirements before you apply.

## What You Get

- Monthly scholarship payments
- Tuition fees paid
- Research allowances for the thesis
- Support from the DAAD and the host university

*Exact amounts and the number of In-Country and In-Region awards differ by programme; check the official call.*

## How to Apply

1. Open the [DAAD scholarship page](https://www.daad.de/go/en/stipa10000486) and choose your Ghana programme
2. Download the programme's **Call for Scholarship Applications** and read the requirements
3. Prepare your documents and apply to the programme as the call instructs
4. Submit before **12 November 2026**

## Tips

- Do not leave this until the last day. The call asks for specific documents and some need certification.
- Apply for admission to the host programme in parallel, since you need to meet its admission requirements.
- Make sure your research interest matches the programme focus.
