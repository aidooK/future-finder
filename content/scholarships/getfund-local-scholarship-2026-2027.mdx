## About This Opportunity

The **Ghana Education Trust Fund (GETFund)** is accepting applications for its **2026/2027 Local Scholarship**, offering financial support to students pursuing tertiary education in Ghana. The programme covers **Diploma, HND, Bachelor's, Master's and PhD** students enrolled in accredited tertiary institutions who meet the requirements and provide the necessary supporting documents.

The application window opened on **5 October 2026** and closes on **27 November 2026**. Applications go through the official GETFund scholarship portal only.

> 📌 Review the [official GETFund announcement](https://www.getfund.gov.gh/news/getfund-opens-applications-for-2026-27-undergraduate-and-postgraduate-local-scholarships) and guidelines for the exact funding coverage and conditions. Third-party summaries report that the local scholarship covers tuition fees only.

## Eligibility Requirements

- Enrolled in an accredited tertiary institution in Ghana
- Pursuing an eligible Diploma, HND, Bachelor's, Master's or PhD programme
- An admission letter from an accredited tertiary institution
- Academic transcripts and certificates
- A valid national identification document, such as the Ghana Card or passport
- All other required supporting documents

## What You Will Do

- Complete the online GETFund Local Scholarship application
- Provide accurate personal and academic information
- Upload the required supporting documents
- Review your application so every detail is correct
- Submit for vetting and shortlisting

## Benefits

- Financial support for eligible tertiary students in Ghana
- Support for undergraduate and postgraduate studies at accredited institutions

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official portal: [scholarships.getfund.gov.gh](https://scholarships.getfund.gov.gh/auth/login)
2. Create an account or log in
3. Complete the online application form
4. Upload your admission letter, transcripts, certificates, valid national ID and any other required documents
5. Review carefully and submit before the deadline

## Tips

- Scan all documents clearly before you start so the upload is quick.
- Use the same name spelling on your Ghana Card, transcripts and admission letter.
- Submit at least two days before 27 November so you can fix any portal issues.
- Apply only through the official portal; never pay anyone to apply for you.
