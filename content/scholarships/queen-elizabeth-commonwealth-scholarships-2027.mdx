The Association of Commonwealth Universities (ACU) is accepting applications for the **Queen Elizabeth Commonwealth Scholarships (QECS) 2027**, supporting two-year Master’s degrees in low- and middle-income Commonwealth countries.

### Benefits
- 100% full tuition coverage.
- Monthly stipend for living expenses throughout the degree duration.
- Economy return flights and one-time setup/arrival grant.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Citizen or permanent resident of a Commonwealth country.
- Completed an undergraduate degree with upper second-class honors or equivalent.
- Applying to an eligible Master's program outside your home country.

### How to Apply
Submit your application through the official ACU portal prior to the November 24 deadline.
