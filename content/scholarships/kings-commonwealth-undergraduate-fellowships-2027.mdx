The Association of Commonwealth Universities (ACU) is accepting applications for **The King’s Commonwealth Fellowship Programme (Undergraduate Fellowships 2027)**, supporting exceptional students from eligible Commonwealth Small Island Developing States.

### Benefits
- 100% full tuition fee coverage for standard program duration.
- Living stipend tailored to the study location.
- One economy-class return flight per year where regional travel is required.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Hold citizenship or recognized refugee status in an eligible Commonwealth nation.
- Commencing full-time undergraduate studies between January and December 2027.
- Demonstrated commitment to serving your local community post-graduation.

### How to Apply
Submit your application through the official ACU portal (MyACU) prior to October 14.
