## About This Opportunity

The **Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at Sciences Po** supports talented young people from the African continent to do a **two-year master's degree** at Sciences Po in Paris. The programme is designed to cover the full financial needs of selected scholars and to support them through their studies.

You make **one application**: a master's application at Sciences Po that is also considered for the scholarship. You must first be accepted onto a master's programme at one of Sciences Po's graduate schools.

## Key Dates

- **23 November 2026:** deadline if you want your master's admission decision by 11 February 2027
- **14 December 2026:** deadline if you are happy to receive your decision by 1 April 2027
- **April 2027:** results of the Mastercard Foundation Scholars selection are communicated, whichever date you applied by

## Eligibility Requirements

- Citizenship of an African country (dual citizenship with a non-African country is excluded)
- A bachelor's degree, completed or being completed, as set out on the official page
- Admission to an eligible two-year master's programme at Sciences Po
- Current Sciences Po graduate students cannot apply

## What You Get

- Full funding for the two years of your master's
- Support designed around a stipend, accommodation and return airfare, according to published summaries; confirm the exact package on the official page
- Access to a community of Mastercard Foundation Scholars

## How to Apply

1. Read the [official programme page](https://www.sciencespo.fr/students/en/fees-funding/bursaries-financial-aid/mastercard-foundation-scholarships/graduate-study/) and check the list of eligible institutions and programmes
2. Create your account on the Sciences Po admissions platform and complete one application for a master's programme
3. Upload all documents and submit before your chosen deadline

## Tips

- If you want an earlier answer, apply by 23 November.
- French is not always required, but check the language requirement of your chosen master's programme.
- Get your transcripts and references early since some institutions take weeks to respond.
