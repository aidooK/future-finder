The University of Cape Town (UCT), in partnership with the **MasterCard Foundation**, is accepting applications for its fully funded postgraduate Scholars Program for the 2027 academic year.

### Benefits
- 100% full tuition and university registration fees.
- On-campus housing and a comprehensive monthly living allowance.
- Comprehensive health insurance and visa application reimbursement.
- Round-trip travel allowance between your home country and South Africa.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Citizen and resident of an African nation.
- Applying for an eligible postgraduate diploma, Honours, or Master's degree at UCT.
- Demonstrated academic capability, leadership potential, and financial need.

### How to Apply
Applications must be submitted through the official UCT MasterCard Foundation portal before October 31, 2026.
