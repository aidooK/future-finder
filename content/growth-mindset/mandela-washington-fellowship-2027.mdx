The U.S. Department of State is opening applications for the **2027 Mandela Washington Fellowship for Young African Leaders**, the flagship program of the Young African Leaders Initiative (YALI).

### Benefits
- Fully funded 6-week Leadership Institute at a top U.S. university or college.
- J-1 visa support, round-trip international travel, and housing.
- Attendance at the annual Mandela Washington Fellowship Summit in Washington, D.C.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Citizens and residents of Sub-Saharan African countries.
- Aged between 25 and 35 at the time of application submission.
- Demonstrated track record of leadership in public management, business, or civic engagement.

### How to Apply
Submit your complete application through the official YALI portal prior to November 10, 2026.
