## About This Opportunity

The **Community Solutions Program (CSP)** is a professional leadership development programme sponsored by the **U.S. Department of State** and implemented by **IREX**. It selects community leaders to spend about **three to four months in the United States** on a hands-on professional practicum with a host organisation, then to return home and lead a community-based **SCALE-Up project** using what they learned.

**Ghana** is on the list of eligible countries for the 2027 programme.

## Eligibility Requirements

- Aged **27 to 39** at the time of application
- A citizen or resident of an eligible country, which includes **Ghana**
- An existing role leading or contributing to community work, with evidence of local impact
- Strong English (finalists take an English language test or submit recent scores)
- Not a current IREX employee, consultant or their immediate family member
- Other criteria set out in the final application materials

## What You Get

- A fully funded programme in the United States
- Orientation, a professional practicum and an end-of-programme workshop (tentative dates: orientation 2 to 5 August 2027, practicum 9 August to 29 October 2027, workshop 1 to 4 November 2027)
- Leadership and project-management development
- Support to implement a SCALE-Up project in your community after returning home

## How to Apply

1. Read the [official IREX CSP application page](https://www.irex.org/program/community-solutions-program-application-information) and the application instructions
2. Prepare your professional background, evidence of community impact and your project ideas
3. Complete the online application before **11:59 PM Eastern Time on 24 November 2026**

## Tips

- Show measurable community results, not just activities. Selection is competitive and merit-based.
- Plan your English test early in case you are shortlisted.
- Write about the specific local problem you want your SCALE-Up project to solve.
