## About This Opportunity

DataCamp is inviting registered users to create a short AI-generated explainer video about any concept related to artificial intelligence or data — and the prize is genuinely unique: a hand-signed, limited-edition Picasso print numbered 110/200. The competition is open to eligible DataCamp users worldwide, requires no advanced coding skills, and is a creative opportunity for students, content creators, AI enthusiasts, and storytellers who can communicate complex ideas clearly in under a minute.

## Eligibility Requirements

- Must be at least 18 years old and eligible to participate in a skill-based competition from your country
- Must have a registered DataCamp account (including DC Donates and Classroom users)
- Must be able to create and submit one original AI-generated video meeting the competition requirements
- One submission per participant

## What You Get / Benefits

- **Grand prize: hand-signed, limited-edition Picasso print** numbered 110/200
- Public recognition by DataCamp on LinkedIn and Instagram
- Portfolio piece demonstrating creative AI communication skills
- Visibility as an AI content creator to DataCamp's global audience

## How to Apply

1. Visit the competition page at the link below and click **Enter Now** to access the competition template
2. Create a **10–60 second AI-generated explainer video** about a concept related to AI or data
3. Begin your video with DataCamp's required intro clip (provided in the competition template)
4. Upload your video to your DataCamp DataLab competition workbook
5. Post the video publicly on **LinkedIn or Instagram**, tag **@DataCamp**, include **#AIForCreativity** and **#WinAPicasso**
6. Submit before **October 9, 2026 at 09:00 UTC**

## Tips

- Pick a concept you genuinely understand — judges score on Clarity, Creativity, and Effective Use of AI. A simple concept explained brilliantly beats a complex one explained poorly.
- Free AI video tools like Runway, Pika, or Kling work for this competition — you do not need expensive software.
- The social media post is part of the submission — post it before you submit the competition workbook, and double-check the tags are correct.
