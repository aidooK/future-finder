Yale University’s Jackson School of Global Affairs is inviting applications for the **Yale World Fellows Program 2027**. This 4-month residential fellowship targets mid-career leaders across the globe to build cross-disciplinary impact.

### Benefits
- Full residential program support at Yale University.
- Housing and living stipend for the duration of the program.
- Comprehensive health insurance and round-trip travel coverage.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Mid-career professionals (typically 5 to 25 years into their career).
- Citizens of countries other than the United States.
- Fluent in English with a proven record of leadership and local/global impact.

### How to Apply
Applications must be submitted directly through the online Yale portal before December 2, 2026.
