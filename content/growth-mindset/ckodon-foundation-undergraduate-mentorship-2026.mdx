## About This Opportunity

The Ckodon Foundation is a Ghanaian nonprofit founded by Selorm Promise Abedu, a KNUST graduate now studying at the University of Rochester in the US. After navigating the scholarship process himself, Selorm built Ckodon to help other talented Ghanaian students do the same — for free. The foundation covers SAT and GRE registration fees for qualified students, provides mentorship through the full university and visa application process for schools in the US, UK, and Canada, and connects students to internship opportunities. This is a verified, well-known Ghanaian organisation featured on Joy News with a track record of students admitted to institutions including Harvard, MIT, and Oxford.

## Eligibility Requirements

- Ghanaian students (or students in Ghana)
- Academically strong — the foundation targets high-potential students who lack financial access to consultation services
- Applicants for undergraduate programmes in the US, UK, or Canada
- Graduate applicants also supported through a separate programme
- Genuine commitment to the process

## What You Get / Benefits

- SAT or GRE registration fee paid by the foundation
- Free, personalised university application mentorship
- Guidance through the visa application process at no cost
- Access to internship mentorship in collaboration with Ckodon Tech
- Support from a community of alumni who have gained admission to top global universities

## How to Apply

1. Visit the undergraduate application portal at the link below
2. Complete the sync.ckodon.com application form
3. Applications reviewed on a rolling basis
4. Shortlisted students will be contacted for mentorship matching

## Tips

- Be honest about your academic background and ambitions — Ckodon selects based on potential and commitment, not perfection.
- Check the Ckodon Foundation's social media and website regularly — they post SAT preparation resources, deadlines, and cohort updates.
- The graduate school programme is also available — if you are a final-year student or recent graduate targeting a Master's or PhD, apply to that track too.
