Two continental heavyweights collide. Argentina face Egypt in the **Round of 16 at Mercedes-Benz Stadium, Atlanta**, kicking off at **12:00 PM ET / 5:00 PM UK time, Tuesday, July 7, 2026**.

## What's at Stake

Argentina, the reigning champions, needed extra time to see off a spirited Cape Verde side 3-2 in the Round of 32, with Lionel Messi opening the scoring before Cristian Romero's 111th-minute header sealed it. Egypt made history of their own — beating Australia 4-2 on penalties after a goalless extra time, securing their first-ever World Cup knockout win and eclipsing their previous best World Cup finish set back in 1934.

This is Messi against Mohamed Salah — two of the greatest attacking players of their generation, on opposite sides, in a win-or-go-home tie.
