## About This Opportunity

The AI Societal Impact Lab has launched its inaugural Autumn 2026 Fellowship — a fully remote, 8–10 week programme designed for recent graduates and early-career researchers who want to critically examine how artificial intelligence is reshaping society, politics, governance, and everyday life. This fellowship is not about building AI systems — it is about understanding their consequences and contributing your voice to one of the most important global conversations of our time. Fellows commit approximately four hours per week, making it compatible with other studies or work.

## Eligibility Requirements

- Recent graduates or early-career researchers
- Background in social sciences or humanities preferred, but STEM, arts, and other backgrounds welcome
- No prior AI technical experience required
- Open to applicants worldwide
- Strong interest in the social, political, or ethical dimensions of AI

## What You Get / Benefits

- Mentorship from AI Societal Impact Lab researchers
- Participation in a collaborative group research project
- Opportunity to publish an original analytical article on the Lab's website
- Weekly workshops, seminars, and discussions
- Access to an international community of researchers and thinkers
- Credential and publication to strengthen your academic and professional profile

## How to Apply

1. Visit the application form at the link below
2. Complete the online application (background, interests, motivation)
3. Submit before September 4, 2026 — applications reviewed on a rolling basis
4. Shortlisted candidates will receive programme details and final schedule

## Tips

- Apply immediately — spots are limited and rolling review means early applicants have an advantage.
- You do not need to be a tech expert. The fellowship values diverse perspectives — social science, law, journalism, policy, and arts backgrounds are all welcome.
- Treat the published article as a portfolio piece — writing about AI's societal impact is increasingly valued by employers, graduate schools, and NGOs.

**⏰ Deadline: September 4, 2026 — or whenever all spots are filled. Apply now at the link above.**
