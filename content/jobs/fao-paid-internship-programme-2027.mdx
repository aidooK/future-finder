The Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO) is accepting applications for its global **Internship Program 2027**.

### Benefits
- Monthly allowance/stipend up to **USD $700** (or equivalent local currency).
- Coverage under the FAO medical and accident insurance policy.
- Mentorship from UN technical specialists in agriculture, economics, and climate resilience.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Aged between 21 and 30 at the time of application start.
- Enrolled in an undergraduate or graduate degree program or recent graduate (within 2 years).
- Working knowledge of at least one official FAO language (English, French, Spanish, Arabic, Chinese, or Russian).

### How to Apply
Create a profile on the UN FAO iRecruitment platform and apply directly before November 30, 2026.
