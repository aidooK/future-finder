## About This Opportunity

FHI 360 is one of the world's leading international development organisations, operating in public health, education, and social development across 60+ countries. Their Ghana office is hiring a Laboratory and Diagnostics Officer to support health programme implementation — a role that combines technical laboratory expertise with public health programme delivery. This is an excellent opportunity for laboratory scientists or public health professionals who want to grow their career within a major international NGO.

## Eligibility Requirements

- Degree in Medical Laboratory Science, Biomedical Science, or a related field
- Professional registration with the relevant Ghanaian licensing body (e.g. AMLS or equivalent)
- Experience in laboratory work within a clinical, public health, or NGO setting
- Knowledge of laboratory quality management systems and diagnostics protocols
- Strong data recording and reporting skills

## What You Get / Benefits

- Competitive salary aligned with international NGO pay scales
- Exposure to FHI 360's global public health programmes in Ghana
- Professional development within an internationally recognised organisation
- Career progression in global health and international development
