## About This Opportunity

**Linfra Ghana**, a member of the **IPT PowerTech Group**, is hiring a **Telecom Civil Site Engineer** in the East Gonja Municipal District, Savannah Region. The successful applicant will join the operations team to supervise civil construction works, ensure quality and safety standards, and oversee the execution of telecommunications infrastructure projects.

## What You Will Do

- Oversee telecommunications tower construction and equipment shelter installation
- Monitor construction quality and supervise contractors
- Enforce health, safety and environmental standards
- Prepare work plans, schedules and progress reports

## Eligibility Requirements

- Bachelor's degree in civil or structural engineering, **or** HND in a related field
- **3–5 years** of practical experience in civil works and structural supervision; telecommunications or tower construction experience is preferred
- Proficiency in **AutoCAD** and project management tools
- Willingness to travel and work at project sites across Ghana

## What You Get / Benefits

- Competitive monthly salary
- Full-time employment
- Opportunity to work on telecommunications infrastructure projects
