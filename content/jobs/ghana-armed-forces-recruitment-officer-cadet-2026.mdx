## About This Opportunity

The Ghana Armed Forces (GAF) has opened its 2026/2027 recruitment for Officer Cadets and Regular Career Officers. This is an official government recruitment with a structured four-step online application process. Two entry routes are available: the **Regular Officer Cadet Course** for candidates with BECE, WASSCE, or SSSCE qualifications, and the **Regular Career Officer Course** for university graduates. Applications close **September 23, 2026** and the portal opened September 4, 2026.

## Eligibility Requirements

**General Requirements (All Applicants):**
- Ghanaian citizen of good character
- Medically fit according to GAF standards
- Able to pass the Pre-Joining Fitness Test
- Unmarried and not bonded
- Age: 18–26 years old by February 18, 2027

**Height Requirements:**
- Male: Minimum 1.68m (5'6")
- Female: Minimum 1.60m (5'2")
- Military Police: 1.75m (male) / 1.70m (female)

**Academic Requirements (Officer Cadet Course):**
- BECE: Six credits (Grades 1–6) including English, Mathematics, and Science
- WASSCE: Six credits (A1–C6) including Core English, Core Maths, and Core Science
- SSSCE: Six credits (A–D) including required core subjects

**Regular Career Officer Course (Degree Holders):**
- First degree with minimum Second Class Lower from an accredited university
- Eligible fields: Engineering, Cyber Security, Information Systems, Supply and Transport, Education, Public Relations, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, and other listed specialisations
- Proficiency in French, Spanish, Chinese, Arabic, German, or Swahili is an added advantage

## How to Apply

1. **Purchase an Electronic Voucher (GH₵200)** from designated Ghana Post offices across all regions (Accra, Kumasi, Tamale, Cape Coast, Ho, Takoradi, and more — full list on GAF website)
2. **Access the portal** at [apply.mil.gh](https://apply.mil.gh) using Mozilla Firefox or Google Chrome — enter your card Serial Number and PIN, select your Arm of Service and Type of Commission
3. **Complete Page 1** — Select vacancy, personal information, and screening centre (Southern/Accra or Northern/Kumasi)
4. **Complete Page 2** — Biodata, passport photo (200×180 pixels), valid email, and mobile number
5. **Complete Page 3** — WASSCE/SSCE results (Core Maths, English, Science), WASSCE Index Number, Certificate Serial Number, tertiary institution details, and upload required documents (PDF only, max 1MB each)
6. **Preview and Submit** — review all details carefully before final submission
7. **Save your GAF ID Number** — received by SMS and email after submission. Print your application summary report.

## Tips

- Purchase your electronic voucher **before** going to the portal — you cannot begin the application without the Serial Number and PIN from the physical voucher.
- Keep the used voucher safe — it will be inspected at **every stage** of the selection process.
- Use only **Mozilla Firefox or Google Chrome** — other browsers may cause issues on the GAF portal.
- Degree holders in Data Science, AI, Cyber Security, and Engineering are particularly sought for the Career Officer route — if this is your background, this is a strong opportunity.
