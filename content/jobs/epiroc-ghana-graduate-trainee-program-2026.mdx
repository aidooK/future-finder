## About This Opportunity

**Epiroc** is a global productivity and sustainability partner for the mining and infrastructure industries, present in about 150 countries. It develops and provides innovative, safe equipment such as drill rigs, rock excavation and construction equipment, and tools for surface and underground applications, along with aftermarket services and solutions for automation, digitalisation and electrification. In Ghana, Epiroc operates through Epiroc West Africa, with its head office in Accra and an office in Obuasi.

**Epiroc Equipment Ghana Ltd** is inviting applications from recent engineering graduates for its **2026 Graduate Trainee Program**, based in **Obuasi, Tarkwa and New Abirem**. The programme offers practical, hands-on experience and the chance to develop technical skills while learning from industry professionals.

## Available Disciplines

- Electrical Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering
- Mechatronics Engineering
- Geological Engineering
- Metallurgical Engineering
- Mining Engineering

## Eligibility Requirements

- A degree or HND in one of the disciplines above
- Completion of the mandatory **one-year National Service**
- Willingness to work in Obuasi, Tarkwa or New Abirem

## What You Will Gain

- Practical, hands-on industry experience
- Opportunities to develop technical skills
- Learning from experienced professionals in the mining and infrastructure sectors

## How to Apply

1. Send your **CV and cover letter** to **EpirocWestAfrica.Career@epiroc.com**
2. Use this subject format: **"Graduate Trainee Program, GHANA – [Your Engineering Discipline]"**

> ⚠️ Remember to include your engineering discipline in the email subject. Applicants must have completed National Service and be willing to work in Obuasi, Tarkwa or New Abirem.

## Tips

- Copy the subject format exactly and replace the bracket with your discipline (for example, "Mining Engineering").
- State your National Service completion clearly in your cover letter.
- Say which of the three locations you could start in, and that you are happy to be posted to any of them.
