## About This Opportunity

**KeyMik** is an AI-powered digital marketing agency based in Atlanta, GA. It connects brands with creators, automates outreach, and delivers measurable results through data-driven, scalable marketing strategies. KeyMik is hiring a **Virtual Assistant / Administrative Assistant** to support its remote team across administration, marketing, sales, recruitment and customer service. The role is open to applicants **worldwide**.

> ⚠️ **Read the terms before you apply.** This is an **independent contractor** position, not a salaried employee role, and the advertised pay is **USD 300 per month** plus annual bonuses. Confirm the contract terms before sharing personal documents, and never pay a fee to apply.

## Job Details

- **Location:** Remote (worldwide)
- **Contract:** Full-time, independent contractor
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM **Eastern Time** (40 hours a week)
- **Start date:** ASAP
- **Deadline:** not stated, so apply early

**Ghana time difference:** Ghana is on GMT all year. During US standard time (roughly November to March) 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern is **2 PM to 11 PM in Ghana**. During US daylight saving time it is **1 PM to 10 PM**. Plan for evening work.

## What You Will Do

- Manage emails, calendars, meetings and appointments
- Organise documents, spreadsheets and company records
- Conduct online research, data entry and reporting
- Assist with project coordination and task tracking
- Prepare meeting notes, presentations and documentation
- Support teams across administration, marketing, sales, recruitment and customer service

## Requirements

- Excellent written and verbal English
- Strong organisational and time-management skills
- Attention to detail and the ability to work independently
- A reliable computer and high-speed internet connection
- Previous administrative or virtual assistant experience is preferred

**Added advantage:** familiarity with Google Workspace, Microsoft Office, Canva, project management tools, CRM software and AI tools.

## Benefits

- Pay: USD 300 per month plus annual bonuses
- Remote work from anywhere in the world
