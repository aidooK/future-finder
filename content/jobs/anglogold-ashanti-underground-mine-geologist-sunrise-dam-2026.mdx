## About This Opportunity

If you love rocks, data and working where the ore actually is, this one is for you. **AngloGold Ashanti** is hiring an **Underground Mine Geologist** at its **Sunrise Dam Gold Mine in Western Australia**, a role at the heart of one of the most geologically complex underground gold operations in the state. You will report to the Senior Mine Geologist and work in a close-knit, supportive team.

AngloGold Ashanti is a global gold mining company with operations, projects and exploration activities across 10 countries on four continents.

> ⚠️ **Read this before you apply:** the role requires **full Australian working rights**. If you do not already hold the right to work in Australia, you are not eligible for this vacancy.

## What You Will Do

- Map underground development drives and assess them for ore and waste
- Coordinate with geology and mining teams to deliver the mine plan on time
- Report on daily, weekly and monthly production against schedule
- Update orebody domains using mapping and drilling data
- Manage drill samples and QAQC of assay results
- Contribute to drill database management, mine-to-mill reconciliation and material tracking
- Manage reverse circulation drilling, from scheduling and design to implementation
- Help grade control geologists build high-quality models using conditional simulation
- Train graduate geologists
- Work safely at all times and follow all safety instructions and procedures

## Eligibility Requirements

- A relevant tertiary degree in **Geology**
- **2 years** of mine geology experience and comfort in underground environments
- A strong sense of safety and a thorough understanding of the mining cycle
- Good communication and writing skills
- **Full Australian working rights**
- A current **C class manual driver's licence**
- Ability to obtain a **National Police Clearance**

The company encourages people to apply even if they do not meet every requirement, so a near match is still worth a conversation.

## What You Get / Benefits

- Fully paid top-tier Gold Cover private health insurance for you and your family
- Flexible working policy
