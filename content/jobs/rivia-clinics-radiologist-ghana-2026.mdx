## About This Opportunity

**Rivia Clinics** is a growing multi-location, multi-specialty healthcare chain providing virtual and in-person care across Ghana. The Radiologist will provide member-centred diagnostic imaging services, ensure accurate interpretation of imaging studies, and contribute to quality clinical care and operational improvement.

## What You Will Do

- Perform and interpret X-rays, CT scans, ultrasounds and other imaging studies
- Collaborate with physicians, surgeons and other healthcare professionals
- Communicate imaging results clearly and compassionately to members and families
- Ensure imaging procedures meet safety standards and best practices
- Maintain accurate imaging reports in electronic health systems
- Mentor junior radiology staff and contribute to continuous clinical improvement

## Eligibility Requirements

- MBChB or equivalent medical degree with **specialisation in radiology**
- Current, valid licence with the **Ghana Medical and Dental Council**
- Minimum **5 years** of post-qualification radiology experience
- Strong expertise in diagnostic imaging and interpretation
- Proficiency in electronic health records and imaging software
- Strong communication, organisational and time-management skills
- Proficiency with Google Workspace, cloud applications and research tools

## What You Get / Benefits

- Opportunities for professional advancement and leadership
- Continuous training, mentorship and exposure to diverse cases
- Opportunity to contribute to healthcare innovation in emerging markets
- Potential opportunities to transition from locum to full-time employment when required
- Participation in a progressive team member options pool
