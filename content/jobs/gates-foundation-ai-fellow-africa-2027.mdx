## About This Opportunity

The **Gates Foundation** is recruiting **AI Fellows** for its **Africa regional offices**. Each fellow is assigned one or two high-priority projects that help advance the foundation's mission, using AI to support evidence-based interventions, strengthen public systems and improve decision-making across African countries. Example project areas include decision-support tools that bring together financing systems across several countries.

The fellowship runs for **12 months**. Applications for the Africa cohort close on **11 November 2026**.

> ⚠️ **Location and work-rights check.** Fellows are based in **Abuja (Nigeria), Addis Ababa (Ethiopia), Johannesburg (South Africa) or Nairobi (Kenya)**, and you must be **legally eligible to work in your chosen country without visa sponsorship**. If you are a Ghanaian without work rights in one of these countries, you are not eligible for this round.

## What You Will Do

- Build AI-powered tools for health, development and education challenges
- Work on one or two high-priority projects over the 12 months
- Communicate technical findings to technical and non-technical audiences
- Adapt your technical approach as project needs change

## Eligibility Requirements

- Strong, demonstrated experience applying AI or machine learning to real problems
- Legal work eligibility in the Africa office location you choose, without visa sponsorship
- The qualifications set out in the official job posting (relevant degrees in computer science, data science, engineering or applied mathematics are typical for this fellowship)

## How to Apply

1. Open the Gates Foundation careers site: [gatesfoundation.org/about/careers](https://www.gatesfoundation.org/about/careers)
2. Search for **AI Fellow, Africa Regional Offices** (listed under position number B021830) and read the full description
3. Complete the online application through the foundation's recruitment portal before **11 November 2026**

## Tips

- Confirm your work-rights status in one of the four cities before you spend time on the application.
- Show shipped work: models, tools or analyses that were actually used, not just coursework.
- Apply only through the official Gates Foundation careers site.
