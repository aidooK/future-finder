The African Development Bank Group (AfDB) is inviting applications for its prestigious **Internship Program 2027**. The initiative offers master's students and recent graduates the opportunity to gain valuable experience in international development finance.

### Benefits
- Monthly allowance/stipend.
- Coverage under the Bank’s medical insurance scheme.
- Hands-on exposure to high-impact development projects across the continent.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Be a citizen of an AfDB regional or non-regional member country.
- Aged 14 to 33 at the time of application.
- Enrolled in or recently graduated from a recognized Master's degree program.
- Fluent in English or French.

### How to Apply
Complete your application profile via the official AfDB vacancy portal before the October 12 deadline.
