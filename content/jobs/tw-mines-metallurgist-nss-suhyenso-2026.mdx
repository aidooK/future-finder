## About This Opportunity

**TW Mines** is offering a **National Service (NSS) placement** for a motivated, qualified **Metallurgical Engineering** graduate at its mining site in **Suhyenso, near Obuasi**. The placement gives practical, hands-on exposure to the mining industry.

## What You Will Do

- Support metallurgical activities at the mining site
- Apply academic knowledge to practical mining operations
- Work collaboratively with the technical team
- Learn and contribute to day-to-day operations under supervision
- Carry out assigned duties responsibly and in line with site requirements

## Eligibility Requirements

- Background in **Metallurgical Engineering** or a related field
- Eligible and available for **National Service** placement
- Willing to work at the TW Mines site at Suhyenso, near Obuasi
- Demonstrated willingness to learn
- Good teamwork skills and a strong sense of responsibility

## What You Get / Benefits

- Practical experience in the mining industry
- A real work environment to apply your metallurgical engineering knowledge
- Development of teamwork and professional skills
- Exposure to mining-site operations

## How to Apply

1. Prepare your updated CV
2. Send it via **WhatsApp to 0548570700** ([tap to open WhatsApp](https://wa.me/233548570700))
3. Make it clear in your message that you are applying for the **Metallurgist NSS placement**
4. Submit before the closing date
