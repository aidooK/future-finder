## About This Opportunity

The **West Africa Civil Society Institute (WACSI)** is accepting applications for its **2027 Next Generation Internship Programme (NGIP)**, a six-month, fully funded internship for young graduates and early-career professionals who want practical experience in civil society development, policy influencing and advocacy, knowledge management, monitoring, evaluation and learning, and communications.

The programme runs from **January to July 2027**. It is open to nationals of **Benin, Mali, Cameroon, Chad, Ghana and Nigeria**. Interns from Benin, Mali, Cameroon, Chad and Ghana are placed at the WACSI office in **Accra**; Nigerian applicants are placed at WACSI's Nigeria node.

## Eligibility Requirements

- A national of Benin, Mali, Cameroon, Chad, Ghana or Nigeria
- A recent graduate or early-career professional
- A relevant degree (areas listed include communication, communication for development, international relations, social psychology, marketing and graphic design; the TOR lists the requirements per role)
- Read the full Terms of Reference on the official page for age, experience and role-specific requirements

## What You Get

- A monthly stipend
- Lunch on working days
- Furnished accommodation and return airfare for eligible international interns
- Six months of mentoring and professional immersion at a leading West African civil society institute

> ⚠️ Check the TOR for the conditions attached to the internship. Earlier WACSI calls stated that interns who did not complete the programme had to reimburse the cost of the internship.

## How to Apply

1. Open the [official WACSI call page](https://wacsi.org/call-for-applications-next-generation-internship-programme-5/)
2. Download the full **TOR and application guidelines**
3. Prepare your application and submit it as the TOR instructs
4. Submit before **8 November 2026**

## Tips

- Match your application to one of the WACSI work areas and explain what you would contribute.
- Your CV and motivation should show civic or community experience, not just academic results.
- Read the TOR twice, because this call closes in two days.
