## About This Opportunity

**Droppin Technologies Ltd** is a Ghanaian technology and transport logistics company that connects drivers and riders in real time, handles on-demand bookings, and provides personal transport and car services. It is hiring **Sales Agents for AAC Ghana**, one of Ghana's fastest-growing automotive companies, based in Airport City, Accra.

AAC Ghana wants smart, energetic, results-driven people who are target-driven and audacious, who can connect with prospective clients, close deals and help Ghanaians get their dream cars. This is a **full-time** role.

## What You Will Do

- Communicate with clients and help them find the right cars
- Follow up on leads, calls and online enquiries
- Explain AAC's car import and auction services clearly
- Work with the marketing and service teams to finalise deals
- Maintain strong customer relationships and ensure satisfaction

## Eligibility Requirements

- Aged between **18 and 35**
- Bachelor's degree or diploma
- Confident, persuasive and fluent in English (Twi or Pidgin is a plus)
- Passion for sales, communication and customer service
- Self-motivated and target-driven
- Experience in sales, marketing or customer support is an advantage

## What You Get / Benefits

- Competitive base salary plus bonuses and commissions
- Daily, weekly and monthly rewards for top performers
- Career growth and professional training
- A young, supportive work environment

## How to Apply

1. Send your **CV and cover letter** to **info@aacgh.com**
2. Use the **position you are applying for as the email subject** (e.g. "Sales Agent")
3. Questions? Office: 3rd Floor, City Galleria Mall, behind Accra Mall, Accra
4. Call: +233 53 890 5632 / +233 54 298 0399

> ⚠️ Applications without the position in the email subject will not be considered.

## Tips

- Open your cover letter with a specific sales result or target you have hit. This is a commission-driven role and they will look for proof.
- If you speak Twi or Pidgin, say so clearly; it is listed as a plus.
- You should never have to pay to be considered for a job. If anyone asks for money, walk away.
