## About This Opportunity

JD Link Oil Company Limited, a supplier of petroleum and allied products in Ghana, is recruiting a Filling Station Manager to oversee the day-to-day operations of one of their filling stations. This is a hands-on management role covering safe fuel operations, staff supervision, stock management, customer service, and sales activities. The role suits experienced candidates with a business or management degree who are ready to take on full operational responsibility for a station.

## Eligibility Requirements

- Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Marketing, Procurement, or a related field (minimum Second Class Lower)
- Four guarantors required
- Strong leadership, organisational, and communication skills
- Good customer service and conflict-resolution abilities
- Basic financial management and stock-control experience
- Knowledge of health, safety, and environmental compliance standards
- Basic computer literacy

## What You Get / Benefits

- Management-level role in Ghana's petroleum and allied products sector
- Hands-on operational leadership experience
- Career development in fuel retail and station operations management
