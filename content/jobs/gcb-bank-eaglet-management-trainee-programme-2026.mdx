## About This Opportunity

The **GCB Eaglet Management Trainee Programme** is a structured **24-month** development programme at **GCB Bank Plc**, built to prepare talented graduates for leadership and other critical roles in Ghana's banking industry. According to the programme site, permanent employment is **assured** on successful completion, while progression to management level depends on completing the full 24 months. The programme is managed in collaboration with **Deloitte**.

Applications opened on **2 November 2026** and close on **23 November 2026**.

> ✅ GCB Bank states that it **does not charge any recruitment fees**.

## Eligibility Requirements

- Must have **completed the National Service Scheme** at the time of application in 2026 (evidence required: National Service certificate or attestation)
- Must be **25 years old or younger** at the time of application
- Must be willing and available to work in any GCB business or support function, and at any of the bank's locations across Ghana
- The programme is reported to be open to graduates from **any academic discipline**; confirm on the portal

## What You Get

- A structured 24-month development experience
- Permanent employment assured on successful completion
- A pathway toward management level, subject to completing the programme
- Training and rotation across the bank's functions

## How to Apply

1. Go to the official programme site: [eaglets.gcbbank.com.gh](https://eaglets.gcbbank.com.gh/)
2. Read the eligibility requirements and click **Apply here**
3. Upload your National Service certificate or attestation and the other required documents
4. Submit before **23 November 2026**

## Tips

- Check the age limit first: you must be 25 or younger when you apply.
- Have your National Service certificate or attestation scanned and ready.
- Be ready to say you will work in any branch or support function, anywhere in Ghana.
- Apply only through the official site above; ignore anyone asking you to pay.
