## About This Opportunity

**GWO SEVO Solutions Ghana Ltd** is a commercial growth and customer intelligence company that brings acquisition, conversion, customer experience, data and digital influence together. It is hiring confident, energetic, results-driven **Digital Marketing Agents** for its Contact Center team in **La, Accra**.

Successful candidates will engage assigned leads through outbound calls, social media and other digital channels, promote products and convert customer interest into sales. The role is **full-time**, with an **on-site and remote** work arrangement.

## What You Will Do

- Contact assigned leads and existing customers through outbound calls
- Engage customers through WhatsApp, social media and other approved digital channels
- Promote assigned products, services and campaigns
- Qualify leads and recommend suitable products, explaining features and benefits
- Handle objections and guide customers toward a purchase decision
- Follow up with interested prospects and incomplete transactions
- Record customer interactions, follow-ups and sales outcomes accurately
- Meet daily, weekly and monthly call, conversion and revenue targets

## Eligibility Requirements

- Strong telephone communication, persuasion and sales skills
- Confidence making outbound calls and engaging customers online
- Ability to create interest, overcome objections and close sales
- Target-driven and resilient, comfortable in a contact-center environment
- Fluency in English (proficiency in a Ghanaian language is an advantage)
- Experience in telesales, contact-center sales, digital marketing, social selling or customer acquisition is an advantage

## What You Get / Benefits

- A target-driven contact-center environment with on-site and remote flexibility
- Performance measured on call activity, conversion rate, sales and quality of customer engagement

*The advert does not state a salary, so ask about pay structure early in the process.*
