## About This Opportunity

**Everyday Items** is seeking a skilled and reliable **Air Conditioning (AC) Technician** to join its team in **Madina, Greater Accra Region**. The successful applicant will install, inspect, maintain and repair air conditioning systems to ensure efficient operation and customer satisfaction. This is a **full-time** opportunity for people with relevant technical knowledge, practical experience and strong troubleshooting skills.

## What You Will Do

- Install, inspect and maintain air conditioning systems
- Diagnose and troubleshoot faults in AC units and related equipment
- Perform routine servicing and preventive maintenance
- Repair or replace faulty components when necessary

## Eligibility Requirements

- Diploma qualification
- **2–3 years** of relevant work experience
- Good technical knowledge of air conditioning systems
- Practical experience in AC installation, maintenance and repair
- Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills

## What You Get / Benefits

- Competitive monthly salary
- Full-time employment
- Opportunity to apply your technical knowledge and practical skills in AC installation, maintenance and repairs
