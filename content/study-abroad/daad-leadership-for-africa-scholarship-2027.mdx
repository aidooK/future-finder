The German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) is offering the **Leadership for Africa (LfA)** scholarship program to support qualified young talents for Master's degree studies at German public universities.

### Benefits
- Monthly stipend of €934 plus travel allowances.
- Comprehensive health, accident, and personal liability insurance in Germany.
- Mandatory 6-month preparatory German language course with accommodation included.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Hold a recognized Bachelor's degree (completed within the last 6 years).
- Good English or German language proficiency (IELTS/TOEFL/TestDaF).
- Open to citizens or refugees residing in selected African target countries.

### How to Apply
Submit your application profile directly on the DAAD portal before October 15, 2026.
