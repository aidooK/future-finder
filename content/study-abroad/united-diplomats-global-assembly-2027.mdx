## About This Opportunity

The **United Diplomats Global Assembly 2027 (UDGA 2027)** is a five-day Model United Nations experience held at the **United Nations Conference Centre in Bangkok, Thailand**, from **25–29 January 2027**. Organised by United Diplomats and powered by Global Peace Chain — an organisation that has conducted panels at the actual UN General Assembly in New York — the event brings together 280 young leaders worldwide to debate and pass resolutions on the theme **"Artificial Intelligence for Peace and Sustainable Development."**

150 of the 280 seats are available through scholarship, making this one of the most accessible international diplomacy programmes for African youth right now. No MUN or public speaking experience is required.

> ⚠️ **Note:** UDGA is an independent educational simulation. It is **not organised or endorsed by the United Nations** — the venue is a commercial booking at the UN Conference Centre. All scholarship applicants pay a **non-refundable $30 application fee** to submit.

## Eligibility Requirements

- Ages **15–35** (applicants aged 15–18 require parental/guardian consent)
- Open to **all nationalities** — no country restrictions
- No prior MUN or public speaking experience required
- Comfortable communicating in **English** (no IELTS/TOEFL required)
- Open to students, young professionals, entrepreneurs, and community leaders from any academic background

## What You Get / Benefits

The Selection Committee assigns your scholarship tier based on your application. The three tiers are:

- **30 Fully Funded seats** — 100% program fee + return airfare + full accommodation (4 nights) + airport pickup + city tour + networking dinner + delegate kit + certificate
- **50 Partially Funded seats** — 70% program fee + 70% accommodation + airport pickup + networking dinner + kit + certificate
- **70 Semi Scholarship seats** — 50% program fee + 50% accommodation + networking dinner + kit + certificate

All delegates also receive:
- Official UN Conference Centre badge and full venue access
- Crisis simulation and leadership masterclass sessions
- Delegate kit and UDGA merchandise
- Certificate of Participation from United Diplomats / Global Peace Chain

## How to Apply

1. Visit **[uniteddiplomats.com/udga](https://uniteddiplomats.com/udga/)** and click Apply
2. Complete the scholarship application form with your personal, academic, and leadership background
3. Pay the **non-refundable $30 application fee** to submit your application
4. Scholarship results are announced on **15 December 2026**
5. If selected, complete your official UN Indico registration at [indico.un.org/event/1025567](https://indico.un.org/event/1025567/) to receive your UN badge
6. Confirm your seat and arrange travel to Bangkok

## Tips

- **Apply well before 30 November** — only 150 funded seats exist across three tiers and competition is global. The $30 fee is required to submit, so budget for it.
- **Your essay is the decision** — the committee evaluates real leadership experience and community impact. Be specific: name programs, numbers, and outcomes, not just aspirations.
- **Sort your passport early** — you need a valid passport plus a Thai visa (unless your nationality qualifies for visa-free entry). United Diplomats issues the invitation letter, but the visa decision is Thailand's — allow 4–6 weeks.
