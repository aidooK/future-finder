## About This Opportunity

**Wageningen University & Research (WUR)** in the Netherlands offers scholarships for talented African students to do an MSc programme starting **September 2027**. You do **not** apply for the scholarship directly. You submit a **complete MSc application** by the deadline, and the selection committees invite eligible candidates into the scholarship process.

Two schemes matter most for Ghanaian students:

- **Africa Scholarship Programme (ASP):** for citizens of an African country who capacity-build back in Africa
- **Anne van den Ban Fund (ABF):** for nationals of listed developing countries, and **Ghana is on the eligible list**

Both cover the same package for the full 24 months of the MSc.

> 📌 The MSc application deadline for both is **before 1 December 2026**. Only complete applications submitted before then are assessed.

## What You Get

- Travel to and from the Netherlands
- A living allowance for the 24 months of the programme
- Tuition fee coverage for 24 months
- Visa costs and health insurance costs

## Eligibility Requirements

- Citizen of an African country (ASP), or a national of an eligible country such as Ghana (ABF)
- Complete MSc application to an eligible on-campus WUR programme submitted **before 1 December 2026**
- **Unconditional admission** to the MSc programme
- An excellent academic record: for the ASP, a **First Class honours degree and a GPA of 80% or higher** in your bachelor's
- English language requirements met, with your official test results available before 1 December 2026
- Bachelor's degree completed, with the diploma uploaded, before 1 December 2026
- For the ASP, preference goes to students who do not yet hold a master's degree

## How to Apply

1. Choose an eligible on-campus MSc programme and read the [WUR application guide](https://www.wur.nl/en/education/master/application-admission-masters/apply-masters-programme)
2. Book and complete your English test early, since results can take several weeks
3. Submit a **complete** MSc application with all documents **before 1 December 2026**
4. Wait to be invited: around March and April 2027, selected applicants hear by email about the final stage, which includes a lottery among eligible finalists
5. Scholarships are offered on **7 April 2027**; the ABF results follow in May 2027

## Tips

- Do not wait until late November: English test results and your bachelor's diploma must already be uploaded.
- Check the [official scholarship page](https://www.wur.nl/en/education/master/study-expenses-masters/scholarships) before you apply, because the rules are specific and strict.
- Make sure every document is in the application, since incomplete applications are not assessed.
