The Swiss Re Foundation has opened applications for its flagship **Entrepreneurs for Resilience Award 2027**. The program targets innovative social enterprises that build resilience in low-income communities through scalable solutions.

### Benefits & Grant Funding
- Total grant package of up to **$500,000** split among winners.
- Tailored non-financial support, executive coaching, and technical advice from Swiss Re experts.
- Access to global networks and investor showcase opportunities.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Open to social entrepreneurs globally addressing critical community resilience challenges.
- Must have a working prototype or established business model demonstrating measurable impact.
- Focus on financial inclusion, climate resilience, or healthcare accessibility.

### How to Apply
Applications must be submitted directly through the official Swiss Re application portal before the October 4 deadline.
