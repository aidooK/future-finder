## About This Opportunity

The Silicon Valley Fellowship is a competitive, week-long immersion programme held in the San Francisco Bay Area, bringing together ambitious founders, students, graduates, and technology professionals from across the world. The Fall 2026 cohort runs November 11–15, 2026, with a final application deadline of October 11. The programme includes workshops, visits to major tech companies and universities, networking events, and talks from experienced Silicon Valley practitioners. With over 300 alumni from 40+ countries — including alumni connected to Apple, NASA, Stanford, MIT, Replit, and Y Combinator — this is one of the most practical global exposure programmes available to ambitious Africans in tech and business. Financial support through the Next Gen Fund is available for admitted candidates who need it.

## Eligibility Requirements

- Open to founders, students, graduates, entrepreneurs, and technology-focused professionals worldwide
- No specific nationality restriction — visa guidance provided for non-US applicants
- Acceptance rate is approximately 5% — strong application required
- Accepted applicants who cannot attend a specific batch can transfer to another cohort
- Applicants requiring financial support can apply to the Next Gen Fund after admission

## What You Get / Benefits

- 5 days of intensive immersion in Silicon Valley (San Francisco Bay Area)
- Workshops, company and university visits, and networking events
- Access to mentors with proven Silicon Valley experience
- Lifetime access to the Silicon Valley Fellowship alumni community (opportunities, referrals, job postings)
- Potential introduction to co-founders, mentors, or employers
- Partial to full funding via the Next Gen Fund (covering tuition, flights, and accommodation) for eligible admitted candidates

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official website at the link below
2. Complete the online application — selection is rolling, so earlier applications get earlier review
3. If accepted and you require financial support, apply to the Next Gen Fund after admission
4. If you cannot make the November cohort, request a transfer to a future batch

## Tips

- Apply now — selection is rolling and batches close when full. The October 11 final deadline is the latest you can apply, not the target.
- The 5% acceptance rate is real — write a sharp, specific application. State exactly what you are building or working on and why Silicon Valley exposure matters to your next step.
- If you are worried about cost, still apply first. The Next Gen Fund exists specifically for admitted candidates who need support — do not self-reject before you even apply.
