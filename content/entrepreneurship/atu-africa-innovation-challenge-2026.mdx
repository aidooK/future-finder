## About This Opportunity

The **African Telecommunications Union (ATU)** is running the **fifth ATU Africa Innovation Challenge**, a continental competition for a **USD 60,000 pool prize**. This edition is open to **academic innovation ecosystems**: innovation hubs, incubation centres, student developer communities and clubs, and research or technical institutions that support innovators building **AI-based software solutions**.

Applicants present both the innovation and the institutional ecosystem behind it. Recognition focuses on the strength of the environment supporting innovators, not just one promising product.

> 📌 **Who can apply:** this is an **institution-level** application, not an individual one. Students and developers should speak to their university innovation hub, incubator, department or developer club about applying. An institution does **not** need to be an ATU member to take part.

## Eligibility Requirements

Each applicant ecosystem must meet **all five** requirements:

- **Institutional connection:** hosted within, or formally connected to, an academic or training institution in an ATU Member State
- **Software focus:** supports innovators working on AI-based software solutions with public, commercial, social or institutional value
- **Named coordination:** has a mentor network, academic supervisor, innovation lead, coordination structure or institutional focal point
- **Evidence of support:** can show how it provides technical, research, product-development or mentorship assistance
- **Learner nomination capacity:** can nominate learners or teams for any partner-supported training or certification pathway

## What You Get / Benefits

| Place | Prize |
|---|---|
| First | USD 30,000 (to be absorbed over one year) |
| Second | USD 20,000 (to be absorbed over six months) |
| Third | USD 10,000 (to be absorbed immediately upon approval of proposed use) |

The prize is mainly delivered as a **support package**, and winners submit a costed support plan that ATU approves. Packages may cover:

- Training and professional certification for eligible innovators
- Technical mentorship, cybersecurity guidance and product-development support
- Software, cloud services, developer platforms and testing resources
- Equipment needed to develop, test or deploy the innovation
- A **cash facilitation equal to 30% of the prize money**, paid to the innovator whose solution the ecosystem fronted

Host institutions may also apply for voluntary ATU academia membership, with a fee waiver of up to three years if admitted. Membership does not affect assessment.

## Key Dates

- **15 September to 31 October 2026:** application period
- **1 to 10 November 2026:** assessment and pitching
- **10 to 20 November 2026:** finalist confirmation
- **7 December 2026:** continental final in Zimbabwe
- **8 December 2026:** winners announced

## How to Apply

1. Confirm your institution's innovation hub or ecosystem meets all five requirements
2. Prepare the host institution details, ecosystem profile, your AI software innovation, its stage of development, evidence of institutional support and the learner pathway
3. Submit through the official ATU challenge page: [atuuat.africa/atu-innovation-challenge-2026](https://atuuat.africa/atu-innovation-challenge-2026/)
4. Read the full [Information Booklet (PDF)](https://atuuat.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/INFORMATION-BOOKLET.pdf) first

## Tips

- Applications are scored on relevance, innovation quality, technical readiness, ecosystem strength and deployment potential. Evidence of **continuing** institutional support matters as much as the product.
- Link your solution to a specific African digital development priority.
- The application window closes on 31 October 2026. If you are reading this later, check the official ATU page for any extension.
