## About This Opportunity

The **Anzisha Venture Building Fellowship** is Africa's flagship programme for its **youngest entrepreneurs**, run by the **African Leadership Academy** in partnership with the **Mastercard Foundation**. It supports founders who have already started a business, project or venture and are committed to growing it. It is **not** for untested ideas.

Fellows join a two-year programme that combines coaching, practical learning and milestone-based financial support, and at the end they compete for a share of prizes across four categories.

## Eligibility Requirements

- Aged **15 to 22**
- A **citizen of an African country** with a valid national ID or passport
- You have **already started** a business or project, with evidence that it is active
- You are the **founder** leading the venture
- Formal business registration is **not** required, but you must show real activity
- Reported time commitment of around 20 hours a week on the venture

## What You Get

- Business development support and coaching
- **Milestone-based cash stipends** during the fellowship
- Financial-readiness training, experiential learning and practical courses
- Shadowing experiences and a peer community of young entrepreneurs from across Africa
- A **USD 40,000 prize pool** across four award categories at the end of the programme
- Membership of the Anzisha alumni network

**Applying is free.**

## Key Dates

- **29 September 2026:** applications opened
- **10 November 2026:** applications close
- **November 2026 to March 2027:** evaluation
- **May 2027:** fellows announced

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official Anzisha site: [anzishaprize.org](https://www.anzishaprize.org/)
2. Confirm your age, citizenship and founder status
3. Prepare evidence of your venture: customers, revenue or users, and what you have achieved so far
4. Submit your application before **10 November 2026**

## Tips

- Show traction: numbers, customers and milestones matter more than a big plan.
- Be clear about your own role as the founder.
- Applications are free. Never pay anyone to help you apply.
