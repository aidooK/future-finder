## A Ghanaian Tech Startup Is Launching — And You Are Invited

**AutoPadi** is officially launching in Accra on Saturday, 15th August 2026, and seats are limited.

If you are passionate about entrepreneurship, tech, or simply want to network with some of Ghana's brightest builders — this is the event to be at.

AutoPadi is a made-in-Ghana app designed to help car owners and drivers maintain their vehicles effectively and access reliable auto services with confidence. Think of it as your personal mechanic in your pocket — built for the Ghanaian market, by Ghanaians.

The launch happens at **ALX Tech Hub** — one of Accra's most active hubs for African tech and entrepreneurship. This is exactly the kind of event where connections are made and ideas are sparked.

- **Time:** 3:00 PM prompt

## What to Expect

- Official unveiling of the AutoPadi app — be among the first to see it in action
- Insights from the founding team on building a tech product for the African market

## Why This Matters for African Youth

AutoPadi is not just a product launch — it is a proof of concept. A team of young Africans identified a real problem (unreliable auto services, lack of transparency in car maintenance), built a solution, and are now taking it to market.

If you are an aspiring founder, this is what the early stages of a startup look like from the inside. Come and learn.
