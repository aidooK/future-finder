The **Tony Elumelu Foundation (TEF)** is inviting early-stage African entrepreneurs to apply for its flagship $100M entrepreneurship initiative providing seed capital, business mentorship, and training.

### Benefits
- **$5,000** non-refundable seed capital grant.
- 12 weeks of intensive online business management training.
- Lifetime membership in the TEFConnect pan-African business network.

### Eligibility Criteria
- Open to African entrepreneurs operating businesses or business ideas within Africa.
- Business must be in its early stage (0 to 3 years of operation).

### How to Apply
Register and complete the business assessment test on TEFConnect before January 31, 2027.
