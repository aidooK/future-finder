## About This Opportunity

The OceanLove Innovation Award is a UN-endorsed global competition now in its third annual edition, inviting innovators worldwide to submit early-stage ideas that protect, restore, or sustain our oceans in line with SDG 14 — Life Below Water. Past editions drew applicants from 38 countries with African innovators among the finalists. The 2026 award offers up to €10,000 in seed funding, a mentorship bootcamp for the Top 30 finalists, and an award ceremony in Los Angeles. You do not need a finished product — an early-stage idea is enough to apply.

## Eligibility Requirements

- Open to solo innovators, students, researchers, small teams, NGOs, nonprofits, and early-stage startups from any country
- Your idea or project must address SDG 14 (Life Below Water) and have raised less than €30,000 in prior funding
- Must be able to participate in the online bootcamp if selected as a Top 30 finalist
- No restrictions on academic background, nationality, or financial capacity

## What You Get / Benefits

- Up to **€10,000 in seed funding** for the winning idea
- Placement in the **Top 30 online bootcamp** — coaching and mentorship from global ocean experts
- Global recognition including media exposure and **UN Ocean Decade visibility**
- Invitation to the **award ceremony in Los Angeles**
- Special Ambassador status for the overall winner
- Access to OceanLove's global network of scientists, NGOs, and ocean conservation partners

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official application page on F6S at the link below
2. Create or log into your F6S account
3. Complete the application form with details about your ocean-focused idea
4. Submit before **September 30, 2026** — no extensions will be granted

## Tips

- Your idea does not need to be technical — campaigns, community tools, policy initiatives, and awareness projects have all made the Top 30 in previous editions.
- In your application, be specific about the ocean problem you are addressing and how your idea creates measurable impact — the judges assess Innovation, Feasibility, and Community Sustainability.
- African applicants have consistently reached the finals — this award actively values ideas from the Global South. Do not undersell your context.
