## About This Opportunity

The **Jim Leech Mastercard Foundation Fellowship on Entrepreneurship — Ghana Chapter** is a fully free, structured fellowship designed to identify, train, and launch the next generation of Ghanaian entrepreneurs. It is a partnership between **Queen's University** (Dunin-Deshpande Innovation Centre), the **Mastercard Foundation**, and local programme leads on the ground in Ghana.

The 2027 cohort is now open for applications. This is one of the few entrepreneurship programmes in Ghana that combines world-class university curriculum, real coaching, and a global alumni network — at zero cost to participants. If you are sitting on a business idea or running an early-stage venture, this is the programme to apply for.

> 📌 **Applications close December 1, 2026. No late applications will be considered under any circumstances.**

## Eligibility Requirements

- **Ghanaian** nationals (resident in Ghana or in the diaspora)
- Current **university undergraduates** or **recent graduates** from universities, vocational institutions, or training colleges in Ghana
- Have a business idea, early-stage venture, or strong interest in entrepreneurship
- Able to commit to the full programme schedule for the 2027 cohort
- No prior business experience required — drive and commitment matter more

## What You Get / Benefits

- **Entrepreneurial skills training** — structured curriculum developed with Queen's University's Dunin-Deshpande Innovation Centre
- **Dedicated 1-on-1 and group coaching** from experienced entrepreneurship mentors
- **Access to entrepreneurial resources** — tools, frameworks, funding guidance, and pitch support
- **Ghanaian and global network** — join a community of fellows and alumni across Ghana and internationally
- **Venture launch support** — practical help to start or grow your business during and after the fellowship
- **100% free** — no tuition, no participation fee, no hidden costs

## How to Apply

1. Click the application link: **[Apply for the 2027 Cohort](https://queensu.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_2skpzGTY6MedJJk)**
2. Complete the full Qualtrics application form — answer all sections honestly and thoroughly
3. Submit before **December 1, 2026** — the system will not accept late submissions
4. Wait for feedback from the Ghana Chapter team on next steps

> The application is hosted on Queen's University's secure Qualtrics platform. You do not need to create an account to apply.

## Tips

- **Do not wait until November** — the December 1 deadline is hard and the programme team have confirmed no extensions. Apply now while details are fresh.
- **Be honest about your idea stage** — you do not need a fully built business. Fellows have been accepted at the idea stage. What selectors look for is clarity of vision, market awareness, and genuine commitment to building something.
- **Mention your community impact angle** — the Mastercard Foundation's core mandate is African youth economic inclusion. Applications that show how your venture will create opportunities for others in Ghana tend to stand out.
