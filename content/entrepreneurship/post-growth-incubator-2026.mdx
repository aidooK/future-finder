## About This Opportunity

The Post Growth Incubator (PGE Incubator) 2026 is a free, nine-week online entrepreneurship programme running from October 13 to December 11, 2026. Designed for social entrepreneurs and startup founders who want to build ventures with genuine impact, the programme introduces Post Growth Entrepreneurship principles — a framework focused on creating businesses that prioritise social and environmental value alongside sustainability. Sessions are held every Friday from 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM CST via Zoom, making it accessible from anywhere in the world.

## Eligibility Requirements

- Social entrepreneurs, startup founders, or aspiring entrepreneurs at any stage
- Interest in integrating Post Growth principles — social impact, sustainability, and purposeful business — into your venture
- Availability to participate in weekly Friday online sessions for nine weeks (Oct 13 – Dec 11, 2026)
- Open to participants worldwide

## What You Get / Benefits

- **Free** participation — no tuition or registration fees
- Nine weeks of structured entrepreneurship training focused on social and environmental impact
- Weekly live sessions via Zoom with other founders and practitioners
- Access to a growing global community of social entrepreneurs and innovators
- Practical frameworks for building ventures that create meaningful change

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official PGE Incubator website at the link below
2. Click the Register Now button on the page
3. Complete the registration form with the required information
4. Submit your application — spots are filled on a rolling basis, so register early

## Tips

- Register now even if October feels far away — rolling admission means early registrants are confirmed first, and spots are limited.
- The Post Growth framework is different from typical startup incubators — it focuses on purpose-driven, sustainable ventures rather than growth-at-all-costs models. If that aligns with your vision, this programme is a strong fit.
- The Friday evening session schedule (3 PM–5 PM CST) is 11 PM–1 AM GMT / 10 PM–midnight WAT — plan your schedule accordingly before committing.
